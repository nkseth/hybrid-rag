# Postmortem: Ledger Billing Delay (March 2026)

On March 1, 2026 the monthly Ledger billing job was delayed by six hours. Customers received invoices late but no invoice amounts were wrong.

Root cause: an expired TLS certificate on the Atlas API gateway caused authentication failures. Because Ledger depends on Atlas for authentication, the billing job retried until it exhausted its retry budget.

Marco Silva was the on-call engineer and led the incident response. Hana Sato renewed the certificate.

Follow-up actions: the Platform team moved all certificates to automatic rotation with Vault, added an alert that fires 14 days before any certificate expires, and added a runbook for billing job retries.
