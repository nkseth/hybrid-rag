# Kestrel Dynamics Company Overview

Kestrel Dynamics is a fictional logistics software company headquartered in Lisbon, Portugal. It was founded in 2019 by Amara Okafor and Tomas Lindqvist. Amara Okafor serves as Chief Executive Officer and Tomas Lindqvist serves as Chief Technology Officer.

The company builds Flightpath, a route-optimization product used by regional delivery fleets. Flightpath plans daily routes for more than 4,000 vans across Europe.

Engineering is organized into three teams: the Platform team, the Routing team, and the Data team. Each team reports to Tomas Lindqvist.

The company's core values are "measure before you optimize", "write it down", and "own the outcome".
