# Engineering Onboarding Guide

New engineers spend their first week shadowing their team's on-call engineer.

Every new engineer gets access to Orion for CI/CD, Vault for secrets, and the Prism warehouse for analytics. Laptop setup instructions live in the internal wiki.

During week two, each new engineer ships a small change to production through Orion. The buddy for Platform team hires is usually Hana Sato; the buddy for Routing team hires is usually Diego Fernandes.

Questions about billing should go to the Platform team. Questions about route quality should go to the Routing team.
